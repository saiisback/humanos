# HumanOS

HumanOS is a responsive conversation for delegating bounded work while keeping human authority visible. A person signs in through a JAW passkey smart account and a backend-verified SIWE session. World ID Proof of Human then establishes a separate, unique human root. Closing root verification keeps the JAW account session; an absent root cannot authorize an ENS agent.

The first composer message creates a mission proposal. The conversation presents the proposed mandate, selected capabilities, expiring ENS agent identity, progress, exact sensitive action review, Jev advice, HumanOS policy, and durable receipts. Creating a mission, authorizing an agent, running it, requesting or cancelling approval, confirming, executing, and revoking remain explicit actions. The selected mission is recoverable through `?mission=`.

JAW identifies the account. World establishes the human root. ENS names and scopes the agent. The backend alone determines whether a protected effect is authorized. A configured service or provider callback is not evidence that an action succeeded. Unknown, absent, rejected and reconciliation-required data remain visibly uncertain. DeepSeek and Jev credentials stay server-side; unavailable integrations are labelled unavailable.

The phone experience uses one conversation column and sheets for missions, identity and connections. Desktop adds a narrow mission rail at wide widths. The generated mobile and desktop composition references and measurable tokens are recorded in [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md).
